            How it works

            Four NVIDIA models.
            One shop's day.

            01

            Nemotron 3.5 Lightning reads every message

            English, chiShona, isiNdebele or all three in one line. Lightning calls typed tools: record a sale, stock received, a cost paid, a customer paying back, a till count. It is fast and costs a fraction of a cent, so every message can use it.

            02

            Nemotron 3.5 Super VL reads the photo

            A supplier's invoice or delivery note becomes structured lines: item, quantity, unit cost, total. Lightning maps them to the shop's own stock list and flags a price that moved or a total that does not add up.

            03

            Nemotron 3 Super thinks it through

            "What should I restock before the weekend?" goes to Super with the whole book and a week of sales. Super also takes over a message when Lightning's tool calls fail twice, so a hard message still gets recorded.

            04

            Nemotron 3 Ultra closes the day

            The owner counts the till. Code works out what the cash should be; Ultra reads the day's records, names the likeliest reasons for a shortfall and writes tomorrow's reorder list.

            The model proposes. Code checks. The owner confirms. Every proposal is built by a small, tested ledger: debits must equal credits, stock moves at cost, prices come from the shop's list unless the owner says otherwise. Nothing is written until the owner taps Record, and every record can be undone.

            Why it matters

            Africa runs on WhatsApp.
            Now its businesses do too.

            Most small shops keep no records or a paper book, so they cannot see their profit, chase what they are owed or show a lender a history. Imali AI is Africa's smartest AI Business OS: one AI agent runs sales, stock, staff, suppliers and double-entry books from a text, voice note or photo, in nine languages. A second agent answers your customers, and payments land in your own account.

            Imali AI was built, tested and piloted with users from April 2026, and launched on 21 October 2026. This hackathon build rebuilds its core loop on open NVIDIA models served by Nebius Token Factory, with the router, the photo reader, the analyst and the day-closer each on the model that fits the job. The shop, names and figures in this demo are invented.
